# 2B room model assets

The GLB assets below are CC0 1.0 assets from Tevy. Their GLB files include the authored geometry and PBR materials/textures. They are locally bundled with the site; the application only fetches the glTF decoding libraries at runtime.

- Desktop monitor: https://tevy.dev/models/desktop-monitor
- Mechanical keyboard: https://tevy.dev/models/mechanical-keyboard
- Gaming mouse and pad: https://tevy.dev/models/gaming-mouse
- Blue enamel mug: https://tevy.dev/models/blue-enamel-mug
- Book row with leaning book: https://tevy.dev/models/book-row-with-leaning-book
- Table lamp: https://tevy.dev/models/table-lamp
- Snake plant in concrete pot: https://tevy.dev/models/snake-plant-in-concrete-pot
- Digital camera with lens and strap: https://tevy.dev/models/digital-camera
- Cappuccino cup and saucer: https://tevy.dev/models/cappuccino-cup-and-saucer
- Wooden-frame sofa: https://tevy.dev/models/sofa

CC0 source note: https://tevy.dev/models

The replacement white leather swivel chair is a measured AmazonBasics product model from the Amazon Berkeley Objects GLB dataset. It contains its authored mesh and PBR textures and is distributed under CC BY 4.0.

- White leather swivel chair: https://huggingface.co/datasets/codepawl/abo-glb/tree/main/objects/B0735X2M1J
- Required credit: “Model by Amazon.com, from Amazon Berkeley Objects, CC BY 4.0.”
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The desk-side paper shredder uses Innerscene's ivory-bin PBR GLB model, made at real-world scale and released as CC0.

- Paper shredder: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/shred-bin-paper-shredder-b68dfbd8

## Additional photoreal surface/model sources

- 24-inch desktop monitor: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/desktop-monitor-24-inch-326dfa17 — CC0; real-scale PBR GLB with glass screen, bezel, electronics housing, stand and base.
- Oak veneer 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_02 — CC0 PBR diffuse, roughness and OpenGL normal maps.
- Oak veneer 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_02 — CC0 scanned oak PBR maps used for the desktop and the individual staggered floorboards.
- Fabric Leather 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_leather_01 — CC0 PBR upholstery leather maps, recolored to the warm ivory reference palette while retaining grain and wrinkle detail.

## Seasonal Korean window view

- `assets/weather/korean-four-seasons.png` is an original generated composite with four matched views of a Korean residential courtyard. The 3D window crops the spring, summer, autumn or winter panel selected by the visitor; the surrounding room and its exterior lighting rig stay in place.
